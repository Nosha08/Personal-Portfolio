About Me

I’m Noah — a recent graduate of Staten Island Technical High School heading to the Wharton School at the University of Pennsylvania in Fall 2026. My work sits at the intersection of computer science, entrepreneurship, and public service. I spend my time building AI-powered systems, running statewide volunteer initiatives, and advocating for small businesses.

On the technical side, I work primarily in JavaScript, TypeScript, and Python, with experience in Vue, Django, and Flask. Most recently I’ve been focused on retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) architectures and NLP pipelines — the kind of applied AI that is actually reliable in production. I care less about impressive demos than about systems that work for real users in the real world.

Outside of code, I’m the New York District Governor of Key Club International (representing 12,000+ members across 250 chapters), Captain of the SITHS Debate Team, and a part-time Business Retention Specialist at the Staten Island Chamber of Commerce. I’m also the founder of Ace of Ages, a history-action board game with national recognition.

At Wharton I plan to study at the intersection of technology, entrepreneurship, and social impact — continuing the work I’ve started here, but at scale.
