SammyGPT

Active
Python TypeScript RAG NLP Django LLM Integration June 2024 – Present

Problem & Purpose

Thousands of immigrant families on Staten Island can't easily communicate with their children's schools because of language barriers. Enrollment forms, attendance policies, IEPs, and event notices are all written in English — leaving non-English-speaking parents locked out of crucial information about their children's education.

SammyGPT was built to dissolve that barrier. It's a retrieval-augmented, multimodal generative AI chatbot deployed across six Staten Island schools, designed to give parents accurate, school-specific answers in their native language — on demand, 24 hours a day.

Technical Approach

  • RAG architecture: Each school's policy documents and handbooks are embedded into a scoped vector store using semantic chunking. Queries retrieve the most relevant passages before generation, preventing hallucination on school-specific facts.
  • Multimodal input: Parents can submit image-based documents (photos of forms, screenshots of notices) for OCR extraction and translation, handled through a Python image processing pipeline.
  • Stack: Python backend (Django), TypeScript frontend, LangChain for orchestration, ChromaDB for vector storage, and a multilingual translation layer for output rendering.
  • Deployment: Presented borough-wide to school administrators; expanded from one pilot school to six in 2024–2025.

Ace of Ages

Active
Entrepreneurship Product Design Market Research Kickstarter 2022 – Present

Problem & Purpose

History education suffers from an engagement problem. Textbooks present events as settled facts — dates, names, outcomes — with little room for the strategic thinking and human agency that actually drove them. Students memorize; they don't reason.

Ace of Ages is a history-action board game designed to change that. Players command real historical factions — Roman legions, Mongol cavalry, Renaissance city-states — making strategic decisions that reflect the actual constraints and opportunities those civilizations faced. The game was built for ages 14+ and reviewed in collaboration with the National Council for Social Studies (NCSS).

Technical Approach

  • Product development: Designed the full game system from scratch — mechanics, faction balance, card economy, and rulebook — iterating through playtesting with 30+ student testers over two academic years.
  • Market research: Conducted competitive analysis of the educational board game market (Ticket to Ride, Pandemic Legacy, Codenames) to position Ace of Ages at the intersection of strategy gaming and curriculum alignment.
  • Fundraising & launch: Designed and launched a Kickstarter campaign including reward tiers, stretch goals, and promotional copy. Collaborated with freelance artists for card and box illustration.
  • Recognition: Won 3rd Place at YEA! Nationals (June 2023); placed 3rd at the Stevens Institute AI Entrepreneurship Competition (Feb 2025).
Press Coverage

Reflection

The biggest lesson from Ace of Ages is that product-market fit is validated by strangers, not friends. Early playtesting with classmates gave me falsely positive signal because people were polite. When I ran structured blind playtests with students who had no prior relationship with me, I got much more honest feedback — including that one of my core mechanics was confusing to new players. I reworked it. If I were starting over, I'd get to strangers faster. I'd also think earlier about manufacturing cost — the unit economics of a physical board game are unforgiving, and we discovered that late.

Staten Island Chamber of Commerce

Active
CRM Systems Policy Research Business Analytics Technical Writing June 2024 – Present

Problem & Purpose

Staten Island's 12,000+ small businesses face persistent economic challenges — rising operating costs, congestion pricing impacts, and limited access to organized advocacy resources. The Chamber of Commerce exists to represent their interests, but doing so effectively requires precise data, compelling documentation, and active relationship management.

As a Business Retention Specialist, my work directly supports the Chamber's mission: keep businesses on Staten Island, help them access resources, and advocate for policy conditions that support their survival.

Technical Approach

  • Accreditation documentation: Authored and structured comprehensive organizational documents for submission to the U.S. Chamber of Commerce as part of an effort to earn the first-ever five-star accreditation for a New York City chamber. This required synthesizing operational data, governance records, and program outcomes into formal frameworks.
  • CRM management: Managed marketing outreach pipelines using the Chamber's CRM platform — segmenting business contacts by industry, size, and engagement history to improve campaign targeting and response rates.
  • Advocacy & research: Contributed to the Small Business Resource Network (SBRN) business visit program and produced research materials for congestion pricing advocacy, analyzing economic impact data and drafting public comment submissions.
